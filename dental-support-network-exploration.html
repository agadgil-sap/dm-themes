Before we work together

A few useful
questions.

What does “qualified” mean here?

It means understanding a person’s enquiry, practical preferences and readiness for a consultation against agreed criteria. A dentist determines clinical suitability and recommends treatment.

How would booking work with our clinic?

We would agree the consultation process, appointment availability and handover with your team. A requested time becomes a confirmed booking once your clinic’s appointment system accepts it.

What would we agree before starting?

Your services, locations, consultation availability, enquiry criteria, communication responsibilities and reporting. Commercial terms would be discussed as part of that conversation.
